HTML5 fan-game demo using fictional credits only. Five reels, three rows, twenty fixed paylines.

How it works

  • Wins pay left-to-right on one of 20 paylines.
  • WILD substitutes for normal symbols.
  • 3 / 4 / 5 SCATTERS award 10 / 15 / 25 Free Spins.
  • During Free Spins, WILD wins receive the active portal multiplier.
  • Portal Instability increases as you spin. Filling it triggers a Portal Surge.
